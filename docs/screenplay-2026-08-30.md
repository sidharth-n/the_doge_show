# The Doge Show — "This Week in AI & Crypto" — 3:20 sample episode (screenplay v1, 2026-08-30)

Purpose: the sample we send Erik + premiere on YouTube. Looks like a real bulletin. Only **3 H3 Max clips**.
Everything else: anchor idle-loop + mouth sprite, HTML/Remotion overlays, real screenshots, charts, stills.

## Always on screen (the frame)
- **Anchor**: 5-s silent idle loop of the Doge studio (blink, tail flick, head locked) — looped invisibly.
  Mouth sprite (closed / half / open) driven by the VO amplitude, positioned on the locked mouth region.
- **Top-left**: THE DOGE SHOW bug + red **LIVE** dot (pulsing) + clock.
- **Bottom**: lower-third (name/topic) above a **ticker** crawl: prices `BTC $78,070 ▲0.5%` … + headlines.
- **Right pane** (when a story runs): "story card" — screenshot / quote card / chart / H3 clip. Anchor shrinks
  to the left third (picture-in-picture) while the card is up; full-frame for anchor-only moments.
- **Breaking bar**: red full-width band with white caps, sting + whoosh, only for the Venice A-block.
- Music: title theme (0–8s, sting), then a low news-bed loop at −22 dB under VO, swells at block changes.
  SFX: sting, whoosh (card in/out), ticker tick, chart up/down, one "wow" bark at the punchline.

## Build stack for the sample
- **Remotion** (we already own the pipeline in `Work/video-engine`): one composition, data-driven from
  `producer/rundown.json` (this screenplay as JSON), all overlays as React components (LIVE bug, ticker,
  lower-thirds, breaking bar, quote cards, price tiles, sparkline), `<OffthreadVideo>` for the idle loop and
  the H3 clips, `<Audio>` for VO/bed/SFX. Render to 1920×1080 MP4.
- VO: `studio/tts.py` (Kokoro am_santa toon-heavy, local, free) per segment → durations drive the timeline.
- Music/SFX: MiniMax Music 2.6 on Venice ($0.18/track: title theme + news bed) or Mixkit/Kenney CC0 (free,
  used in video-engine before). Decide by ear; free first.
- Screenshots: real X/Reddit/Verge posts rendered as styled quote cards from the data (not browser captures —
  cleaner, and credited with handle + date).
